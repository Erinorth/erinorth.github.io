Data Deletion

Instructions for deleting your data in NoSpendDay.

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

Delete a single day's record

  1. Open the NoSpendDay app.
  2. Go to the Calendar tab.
  3. Tap the day you want to remove until it returns to the unrecorded state (grey).

Tapping cycles through: ⬜ Not recorded → 🟢 No-Spend → 🔴 Spent → ⬜ Not recorded.

Delete all data

  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom tab).
  3. Tap Account icon in the top-right.
  4. Select Delete Account to remove all data permanently.

This removes all your records from the server.

Guest mode note

Guest mode has no account data on the server. All data is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
