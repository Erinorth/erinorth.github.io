GrowthDesk

A tool for managing and publishing short-form video content

GrowthDesk helps its developer track performance and publish short videos to their own YouTube channel, connecting through YouTube API Services with the channel owner's consent.

What GrowthDesk Does

  • Uploads videos to the YouTube channel of the owner who grants access
  • Reads public video statistics and comments to analyze which content works
