Data Deletion — PetSym

Instructions for deleting data inside the app.

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Delete a single symptom log

  1. Open the PetSym app.
  2. Navigate to the History screen for the relevant pet.
  3. Tap the symptom entry you want to remove.
  4. Tap Delete and confirm.

This deletes only the selected symptom record.

Delete a pet profile and all its logs

  1. Open the PetSym app.
  2. Go to My Pets.
  3. Tap the pet you want to remove.
  4. Tap Delete Pet and confirm.

This permanently removes the pet profile and all associated symptom history.

Delete all data

  1. Open the PetSym app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Data.
  4. Tap Clear all data and confirm.

This removes all locally stored data from your device.

Guest mode note

Guest mode has no account data on the server. Clearing data in the app removes local data only.
