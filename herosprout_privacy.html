Privacy Policy — HeroSprout

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

HeroSprout is a family app: a parent creates an account, and a child ("Hero") uses a linked profile under that parent's supervision. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and — because HeroSprout is used by children — how we protect a child's information specifically.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Parent account

  • Email address and authentication data (email/password or Google Sign-In)
  • Rewards, quests, and settings the parent configures for their family

1.2 Child ("Hero") account

  • A Hero display name and avatar chosen by the child (no requirement to use a real name)
  • Login credentials (email/password or Google Sign-In) used only to sign back into the same device or a new one
  • In-app game progress: quest completions, XP, coins, hero level, battle results, badges

1.3 Automatically collected information

  • Usage data: features used, session length
  • Device data: operating system, device model

2. Children's Privacy & Parental Consent (COPPA)

A child cannot create a Hero account by themselves. Before any child data is collected:

  1. A parent creates and verifies their own HeroSprout account.
  2. The parent generates a one-time, time-limited consent code from inside the app.
  3. The parent gives that code to their child. The code is required to even reach the child sign-up form, and it is verified by our server before any child account is created.
  4. The resulting Hero profile is automatically and exclusively linked to the consenting parent.

We collect only what is needed to run the game experience for the child: a chosen hero name, an avatar, and in-app progress. We do not knowingly collect precise location data, show behavioral/interest-based advertising, or allow a child to communicate with people outside their own family group.

A parent may, at any time, from the Account page or by emailing us:

  • review what data has been collected about their child;
  • request correction of that data;
  • request deletion of their child's account and all associated data (see our Account Deletion and Data Deletion pages); and
  • refuse further collection by not generating additional consent codes.

If you believe a child has created a Hero account without a valid parental consent code, please contact us immediately at north.affhub@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete the account.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the quest, battle, reward, and progress-tracking features
  • To let a parent monitor and approve their child's activity
  • To communicate with the parent about the account

4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share a child's data with third parties for marketing purposes. We use Supabase (database/authentication) and the app stores' in-app purchase systems (Google Play / App Store) strictly to operate the service; these providers have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as the account is active. When a parent or child account is deleted, associated data is permanently removed (see the deletion pages linked above).

6. Your Rights

  • Access: request to view data we hold about you or your child
  • Rectify: request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erase: request deletion of your or your child's data
  • Withdraw consent: stop issuing new consent codes and delete an existing child account at any time

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures (encrypted transport, row-level access control in our database) to prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure of data.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon publication on this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your or your child's data:

  • Email: north.affhub@gmail.com

This document provides general information and is not legal advice.
